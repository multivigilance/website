Our Services

Complete pharmacovigilance support

Pick a single activity or outsource your whole safety function. We serve multiple client companies, so you get a mature, ready team without the overhead.

01

ICSR Case Processing

Case intake, triage, duplicate check, data entry, MedDRA and WHO-DD coding, narrative writing, quality review and E2B (R3) submission within regulatory timelines.

02

Aggregate Safety Reports

Authoring and compilation of PSUR / PBRER, DSUR, PADER and Addendum to Clinical Overview, with data outputs and line listings.

03

Signal Management

Periodic signal detection, validation, evaluation and documentation from safety databases, literature and regulatory sources.

04

Literature Surveillance

Weekly global and local literature screening, article review, case identification and audit-ready search records.

05

Risk Management

Risk Management Plans, additional risk minimisation measures and tracking of safety commitments.

06

PV System & Compliance

PSMF preparation, SOP writing, QPPV / LPPV support, audit and inspection readiness, and compliance metrics.
